Why Join

More Than A Team

Mountain biking is a rare kind of sport: you race as an individual, but you train, travel, and grow as a team. It's a family sport, a fitness sport, and a fun sport — all in one pack.

SoCal League Racing

Compete in the SoCal High School Cycling League — six spring races on premier courses across Southern California.

Team Family

Ride with friends who become family. Group rides, team dinners, race-day tents — a community that shows up for each other.

Fitness For Life

Build endurance, strength, and confidence on the bike — habits that carry far past the finish line.

All Skill Levels

No experience necessary. Beginners are welcomed and mentored; advanced riders are pushed to grow. Your own mountain bike and helmet required.

Structured Season

Practices twice a week, weekend team rides, and a six-race spring season. Predictable, consistent, and family-friendly.

Where We Ride

Practice Twice A Week

SoCal League Coach Licensing

Two License Levels

The SoCal League licenses coaches at two levels — Assistant Coach and Coach. Everyone starts on the Assistant pathway; after two seasons you must reach Coach level to keep coaching. The more Coach-level licensees a team has, the more resilient it is.
